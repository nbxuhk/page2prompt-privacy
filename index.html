Page2Prompt · Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 27 October 2026  ·  Last updated: 27 October 2026  ·  Version 1.0

Short version: nothing leaves your browser.

Page2Prompt runs entirely on your device. It has no servers, no accounts, no analytics, no tracking and no cookies of its own. The only data it touches — the page you choose to convert, plus that page's title and URL — is read when you click the toolbar icon and is stored only in your browser. If you use Send to AI, that content goes to the service you picked, and nowhere else.

1. Who we are

Page2Prompt ("the extension", "we", "us") is a browser extension for Chrome and other Chromium-based browsers that converts the page you are viewing into Markdown suitable for large language models.

This policy explains what the extension does with information on your device. It applies to the extension and to this policy page only. It does not apply to third-party websites you choose to open, including AI chat services.

2. What the extension does with page content

When you click the Page2Prompt icon, the extension requests temporary access to the active tab, reads the article's content from the page, and converts it to Markdown locally. The conversion code — including the readability extractor and the Markdown converter — is bundled with the extension and runs on your device.

  • Page content is processed in memory and is not transmitted anywhere.
  • The extension makes no network requests. There are no analytics, telemetry, crash-reporting or advertising SDKs.
  • Access is granted per click through Chrome's activeTab mechanism. The extension does not run on every page you visit and does not request access to all sites.

3. What is stored on your device

StorageContentPurpose
chrome.storage.sync Preferences: preferred AI target, whether to include front matter, and similar settings. Keeps your settings between sessions. If you are signed in to Chrome with sync enabled, Chrome — not Page2Prompt — may sync these settings to your other devices. We never see them.
IndexedDB (local) Your last 50 clips: title, URL, timestamp, token estimate and the converted Markdown. Powers the local clip history so you can search and re-export recent clips.

Both stores live in your browser profile. You can delete individual clips, clear the whole history from the extension's History view, or remove all data by uninstalling the extension. We cannot access, retrieve or restore this data because it never reaches us.

4. Clipboard

When you press "Copy Markdown", the converted text is written to your system clipboard. The extension reads the clipboard only if a feature explicitly requires pasting, and it does not monitor or log clipboard contents. Clipboard access is triggered only by your action.

5. Opening third-party AI services

The "Send to AI" feature opens a new tab for the service you select — for example ChatGPT, Claude, Kimi or Gemini. For services that accept pre-filled input through a URL parameter, the converted text is included in that address, which means your browser sends it to the selected service when the page loads. For services that do not support pre-filling, the text is copied to your clipboard and you paste it yourself.

In both cases the transfer is initiated by you, one click at a time, and only to the service you picked. Page2Prompt has no server of its own and does not send your content anywhere else. Once a third-party page is open, that service's privacy policy and terms apply to what it receives.

6. Permissions and why they are needed

PermissionWhy it is requested
activeTabGrants temporary access to the current tab only after you click the extension icon, so the page can be read and converted.
scriptingInjects the bundled extraction script into that tab to read the article body and produce Markdown.
storageSaves your preferences locally and keeps the optional clip history on your device.

The extension does not request host_permissions, tabs, history, cookies, webRequest or any other sensitive permission.

7. Data sharing, sale and transfer

  • We do not transmit, sell, rent, trade or transfer user data off your device.
  • We do not use or transfer user data for purposes unrelated to the extension's single purpose.
  • We do not use or transfer user data to determine creditworthiness or for lending purposes.
  • We do not use user data for personalised advertising, profiling or retargeting.

9. Chrome Web Store Limited Use disclosure

Page2Prompt's use of information received from Chrome APIs adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data accessed through browser APIs is used only to provide the extension's single purpose: converting the page you are viewing into Markdown on your device.

10. Security

Because no data is transmitted, there is no server-side store to breach. All code that handles page content ships inside the extension package; the extension does not load or execute remote code. You can inspect the package contents in chrome://extensions at any time.

11. Children

The extension is a developer productivity tool and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

12. Changes to this policy

If the extension's data practices change — for example, if an optional cloud sync feature is introduced — this policy will be updated before that feature ships, and the effective date above will change. Material changes will also be noted in the extension's changelog and on its store listing.

13. Contact

Questions about this policy or the extension's data practices can be raised at github.com/nbxuhk.
